# Privacy Policy for Pure PDF Viewer

**Last updated:** October 31, 2025

## Introduction

Pure PDF Viewer ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our Android application.

## Information We DON'T Collect

Pure PDF Viewer is designed with privacy in mind. We DO NOT:

- ❌ Collect personal information
- ❌ Track your usage
- ❌ Access your files beyond what you explicitly open
- ❌ Send data to external servers
- ❌ Use analytics or tracking services
- ❌ Display advertisements
- ❌ Require internet connection
- ❌ Store data in the cloud
- ❌ Share data with third parties

## Permissions We Require

### Storage Permission (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE)

**Why:** To allow you to open PDF files from your device.

**How we use it:** Only when you explicitly select a file to open. We do not scan, access, or read any files without your direct action.

**What we DON'T do:** We don't access other files, don't upload files anywhere, and don't store file paths.

## Data Storage

All PDF files you open remain:
- ✅ On your device only
- ✅ Under your control
- ✅ Not copied or backed up by the app
- ✅ Not accessible to us or anyone else

## Third-Party Services

We do NOT use:
- Analytics services (Google Analytics, Firebase, etc.)
- Advertising networks
- Social media SDKs
- Cloud storage services
- Any third-party libraries that collect data

## Technical Implementation

The app uses:
- **PDF.js** (Mozilla's PDF rendering library via CDN)
- **Apache Cordova** (open-source mobile framework)

These are used only for displaying PDF files and do NOT collect any user data.

## Children's Privacy

Pure PDF Viewer is safe for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes to Privacy Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, we will:
1. Update the "Last updated" date
2. Notify users through the app (if applicable)
3. Publish the new policy in the app store listing

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- ✅ Use the app without providing any personal information
- ✅ Uninstall the app at any time
- ✅ Know that no data about you is stored or transmitted

## Summary

**Pure PDF Viewer respects your privacy. Period.**

We don't collect data. We don't track you. We don't show ads. Your files stay on your device. That's it.
