## 🎬 Launch Strategy

### Phase 1: Pre-Launch (Weeks 1-2)

**Objectives:**
- Build anticipation
- Gather early adopters
- Create buzz in developer communities

**Tactics:**
1. **Teaser Campaign**
   - Twitter/X announcements
   - LinkedIn posts
   - Reddit r/programming, r/webdev posts
   - Hacker News submission

2. **Beta Program**
   - Invite 100 beta testers
   - Collect feedback and testimonials
   - Build case studies

3. **Content Creation**
   - Launch blog posts (3-5 articles)
   - Demo videos (product walkthrough)
   - Documentation completion

### Phase 2: Soft Launch (Weeks 3-4)

**Objectives:**
- Test messaging and positioning
- Refine user onboarding
- Gather initial metrics

**Tactics:**
1. **Product Hunt Launch**
   - Prepare hunter outreach
   - Create launch assets
   - Schedule for Tuesday or Wednesday
   - Engage with community

2. **Social Media Campaign**
   - Daily posts across platforms
   - Behind-the-scenes content
   - User success stories

3. **Email Campaign**
   - Announce to beta users
   - Exclusive early bird pricing
   - Referral incentives

### Phase 3: Full Launch (Week 5+)

**Objectives:**
- Maximize visibility
- Drive signups
- Establish market presence

**Tactics:**
1. **Press Release**
   - Tech publications (TechCrunch, The Verge, etc.)
   - Developer-focused media (Dev.to, freeCodeCamp)
   - Press kit with screenshots and quotes

2. **Influencer Campaign** (See detailed section below)
   - 20-30 developer influencers
   - Sponsored content
   - Affiliate partnerships

3. **Paid Advertising**
   - Google Ads (developer keywords)
   - LinkedIn Ads (B2B targeting)
   - Twitter/X Ads (developer audience)
   - YouTube pre-roll (tech channels)
